## Sample chapter 19 (Sun, Earth and Moon)

All source images are NASA works in the public domain.

- earth_side, earth_pole, earth_big: NASA Earth Observatory, "Whole world - land and oceans" (Blue Marble).
- moon_near: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, LRO WAC nearside mosaic.
- moon_far: NASA, LRO farside mosaic.
- sun: NASA/SDO and the HMI science team.
- solar_eclipse: NASA/Carla Thomas, total solar eclipse of 21 August 2017.
- lunar_eclipse: NASA Glenn Research Center / Jordan Cochran, total lunar eclipse of 14 March 2025.
